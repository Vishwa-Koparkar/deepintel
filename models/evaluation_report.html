Selection Reasoning

Why XGBoost was selected:

Both models achieved similar validation accuracy (~85%). XGBoost edged ahead with 85.31% vs Random Forest's 85.23%. Phishing recall is strong at ~95% for both models, meaning the system correctly catches most phishing URLs. Malware recall (~66%) has room for improvement — this will be addressed in Layer 2 (heuristic scoring) and Layer 3 (threat intelligence), which together form the final fusion score. The selected model is saved and ready for integration into the detection pipeline.
