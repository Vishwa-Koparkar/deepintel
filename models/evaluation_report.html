Selection Reasoning

Why XGBoost was selected:

Both models were trained on the same 60,000-URL dataset with 20 features. XGBoost achieved 84.18% validation accuracy vs Random Forest's 84.12%. XGBoost's boosting mechanism focuses successive trees on previously misclassified examples, giving it an advantage on ambiguous URL patterns. Its built-in regularization (subsample=0.8, colsample_bytree=0.8, gamma=0.1) helps prevent overfitting on the larger dataset. The tuned hyperparameters (400 trees, depth 8, learning rate 0.05) with early stopping provide a robust model that generalizes well to unseen URLs.
